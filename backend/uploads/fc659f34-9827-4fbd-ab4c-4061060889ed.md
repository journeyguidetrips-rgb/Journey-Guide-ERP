## Tour Plan
### Day 1: Arrival Srinagar – Transfer to Pahalgam – Night Stay  
Arrival at Srinagar Airport. Meet and greet followed by transfer to Pahalgam. En route visit saffron fields and scenic countryside. Upon arrival, check-in at hotel. Evening at leisure near Lidder River. Overnight stay at Pahalgam.  

### Day 2: Pahalgam Local Sightseeing – Night Stay  
After breakfast, proceed for local sightseeing. Visit Aru Valley, Betaab Valley and Chandanwari (by union cab at own cost). Enjoy the natural beauty, lush meadows and river views. Overnight stay at Pahalgam.  

### Day 3: Pahalgam to Gulmarg – Night Stay  
After breakfast, transfer to Gulmarg. Enjoy scenic drive through valleys and forests. Upon arrival, check-in at hotel. Evening at leisure. Overnight stay at Gulmarg.  

### Day 4: Gulmarg to Srinagar – Night Stay  
After breakfast, enjoy Gondola Ride (both phases included) up to Apharwat Peak for breathtaking views. Later drive to Srinagar and check-in at hotel. Evening free for leisure. Overnight stay at Srinagar.  

### Day 5: Srinagar to Sonmarg – Day Trip  
After breakfast, proceed for a full day excursion to Sonmarg. Enjoy scenic landscapes, Sindh River and surrounding mountains. Optional pony ride to Thajiwas Glacier (at own cost). Return to Srinagar by evening. Overnight stay at Srinagar.  

### Day 6: Srinagar Local Sightseeing – Night Stay  
After breakfast, proceed for local sightseeing covering Mughal Gardens – Nishat Bagh, Shalimar Bagh and Chashma Shahi. Visit Pari Mahal and Shankaracharya Temple for panoramic views. Later enjoy a relaxing 1-hour Shikara ride. Overnight stay at Srinagar.  

### Day 7: Departure  
After breakfast, check-out and transfer to Srinagar Airport for onward journey with beautiful memories of Kashmir.
